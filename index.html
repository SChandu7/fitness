Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our Fitness Application ("App", "we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services. By using the application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Authentication details from Google or Apple login

Health and Fitness Data

To provide personalized fitness recommendations, we collect health-related information such as:

  • Age
  • Height
  • Weight
  • Lifestyle information
  • Fitness goals
  • Workout activity
  • Nutrition information

Smartwatch or Fitness Band Data

If you connect a smartwatch or fitness band, we may access:

  • Steps
  • Calories burned
  • Heart rate
  • Sleep information
  • Workout sessions

This data is accessed only with your permission through health platforms such as Apple HealthKit or Android Health Connect.

Food Scanning Data

If you use the meal scanning feature, images of food may be processed to estimate nutritional values such as protein, carbohydrates, and calories. Images may be temporarily processed for analysis.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide personalized fitness and workout plans
  • Track health and fitness progress
  • Estimate nutritional intake
  • Provide notifications and reminders
  • Improve app functionality
  • Maintain leaderboard and streak features

3. Notifications

The application may send notifications related to:

  • Workout reminders
  • Daily nutrition goals
  • Fitness streak updates
  • Health insights

You can disable notifications anytime in device settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data may only be shared with:

  • Trusted service providers required to operate the app
  • Health platform integrations (Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect)
  • Legal authorities if required by law

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Data Control

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data

7. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third Party Services

The application may integrate with third-party services including:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Apple Sign-In

These services have their own privacy policies.
